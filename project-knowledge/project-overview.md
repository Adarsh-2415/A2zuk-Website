# Project Overview — A2ZUK

## 1. Project Identity & Purpose

- **Project Name:** A2ZUK `[CONFIRMED]`
- **Product Category:** Public-Facing Online Contest Platform `[CONFIRMED]`
- **Primary Geographic Focus:** India (initial launch) `[CONFIRMED]`
- **Approved Visual & Design Baseline:** [Figma Design Reference](https://www.figma.com/design/YbwxzoyhZKLYnW9OSmCH0x) `[CONFIRMED]`

A2ZUK is conceived as an ongoing, trust-centered online contest platform. It provides a structured, engaging, and transparent environment where visitors can discover active contests, explore eligible participant entries, submit nominations/entries, cast public votes, and follow verified contest results.

Rather than a single, disposable promotional microsite, A2ZUK is being built as an evolving contest business platform capable of hosting recurring campaigns and diverse contest categories over time.

---

## 2. Product Vision & Emotional Resonance

The public-facing experience must actively communicate five emotional and operational pillars:
1. **Trust:** Clear rules, honest voting mechanisms, privacy preservation, and visible accountability.
2. **Excitement:** Visually engaging presentations, celebration of participants, dynamic vote displays, and anticipation of contest milestones.
3. **Active Participation:** Low-friction browsing, intuitive entry submissions for non-technical users, and frictionless voting.
4. **Fairness:** Transparent vote counting, anti-manipulation scrutiny, clear criteria, and impartial result verification.
5. **Clarity:** Explicit status communication (upcoming, accepting entries, voting open, voting closed, results pending, completed) so users always understand where a contest stands and what action is required next.

## 5. High-Level User Journey

The overarching visitor flow across the platform follows a logical, self-explanatory progression:

1. **Discovery:** Visitor lands on the A2ZUK platform (via direct navigation, search, or shared links).
2. **Contest Exploration:** Visitor explores active and upcoming contests, understanding their theme, timeline, and current phase.
3. **Contextual Engagement:** Visitor opens a specific contest to inspect rules, eligibility criteria, and key dates.
4. **Entry Exploration:** Visitor browses the gallery of published participant entries and views individual profiles.
5. **Phase-Dependent Action:**
   - *If Submissions Open:* Eligible guardians submit photographs and required declarations.
   - *If Voting Open:* Visitors cast votes for their favorite entries and observe updated vote counts.
   - *If Voting Closed / Results Pending:* Visitors observe closing notices and await result verification.
   - *If Completed:* Visitors review verified winners, final rankings, and archived results.
6. **Retention:** Visitors discover upcoming contests and return for subsequent announcements.
